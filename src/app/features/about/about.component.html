About Kevin Paul Montealegre Melo

Senior Mobile Engineer focused on Android, iOS and scalable digital products

Kevin Paul Montealegre Melo is a Senior Mobile Engineer based in Bogotá, Colombia, specializing in high-performance native mobile applications for Android and iOS. He has experience delivering scalable, secure and user-focused solutions across fintech and enterprise environments, including digital products used by millions of users.

His core expertise includes Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Swift, SwiftUI and modern software architecture patterns such as Clean Architecture and MVVM. He also works across backend integrations with Spring Boot, REST APIs and secure client-server communication for production-grade applications.
